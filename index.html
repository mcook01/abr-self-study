    For families and staff: sources and contacts

    This module explains the New Jersey Anti-Bullying Bill of Rights Act (P.L.2010, c.122, as amended through P.L.2021, c.338) for students in grades 6–8. The definition of harassment, intimidation, or bullying is taken from N.J.S.A. 18A:37-14. Reporting and investigation timelines come from N.J.S.A. 18A:37-15 and N.J.A.C. 6A:16-7.7, and the NJDOE Model Policy and Guidance (August 2022). As of October 2026, the statutory definition has not changed since 2010; the most recent amendments (2022) changed reporting procedures, including the state HIB 338 form.

    Families can report using the state HIB 338 Form for Families/Caregivers (available in 12 languages) or the district's online reporting option, which allows anonymous reports. Your school's anti-bullying specialist and the district anti-bullying coordinator are listed in the district HIB policy (Policy 5512) and on the district website.
