# Tigers Know the ABR

An interactive self-study module for New Jersey middle school students (grades 6–8) on the Anti-Bullying Bill of Rights Act (ABR) and harassment, intimidation, or bullying (HIB).

- Single page: `index.html`. No accounts, no tracking. Student progress is saved only in the student's own browser.
- Fonts are self-hosted in `fonts/` (SIL Open Font License; see `fonts/LICENSES-OFL.txt`).
- Legal content is based on N.J.S.A. 18A:37-14 and 18A:37-15 (as amended through P.L.2021, c.338) and NJDOE guidance, reviewed October 2026.

Prepared for JP Case Middle School, Flemington-Raritan School District.
